Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and the dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Largo Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we schedule visits around your hours.

We keep temperatures and recovery where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork shows a well-maintained facility to inspectors and landlords.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, verify adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

After-Hours Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

Every hour without hot water costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the dish room. For a salon, it cancels appointments. In a hotel, it means complaints at the front desk, and in an apartment building it invites tenant disputes. Largo Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, and we can schedule planned work after hours to keep downtime short.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including gas valve failures, burned-out heating elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, high-limit lockouts, leaking tanks, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, isolating a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

A maintenance program from Largo Water Heater is built around your property's specific equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap. Mineral-heavy water cuts the life of commercial equipment quickly, so busy properties often need more frequent visits.
