Keeping Your Tampa Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush once a year if their water is hard.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually encodes the manufacture date, and that date says a lot about what comes next. Most tank heaters reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units around 20. If your heater is beyond that point and is also showing rusty water, rumbling, or moisture at the base, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement after a leak.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet that same buildup, ignored for a few years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and burn out the lower element on an electric model, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number in your contacts now so help is one call away the moment something seems off. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Hot Water Headaches Tampa Homeowners Face

Whatever type of water heater your Tampa home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units lose elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale buildup, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
